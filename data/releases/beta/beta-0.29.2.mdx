# beta-0.31.1
##### May 16, 2024

## Enhancements
1. [Global Acceleration]: Our Premium tier customers automatically have access to our global acceleration network. This network is designed to reduce latency for your game users across the globe. New SDKs generated after this release will automatically have this feature enabled.
2. [Analytics Graphs]: We have released new default graphs viz. Transactions, Revenue, First Transaction, First Transaction Revenue. You can find these graphs under the Revenue dashboard for your Snapend in the Snapser Business Intelligence tool.
3. [Emitters]: Social graph, profiles, matchmaking and party snaps have now been integrated with the Snapser emitter system.
4. [FTUE]: We released a new on rails first time user experience for the engineering persona.
