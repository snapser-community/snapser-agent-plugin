# beta-0.37.0
##### September 6, 2024

## Snaps
### Quests
- **Reward Tasks**: Quests now support optional rewards at a per task level. This allows you to reward players for completing individual tasks within a quest.

### Matchmaking
- **Team Formation**: Matchmaking now supports team formation. You can now create teams and assign players to teams based on the matchmaking results.

### Inventory
- **Lifetime Earn and Spend per user**: Inventory snap now supports lifetime earn and spend tracking per user. This allows you to track the total amount of currency earned and spent by a user. This feature has a dependency on the Statistics snap.

### Notifications
- We updated the Notification snap to make campaigns more performant. Further improvements to the Notification snap are in the works.
