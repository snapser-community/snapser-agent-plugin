# User Reports

## Concept

The **User Reports** tool lets engineers and customer service agents search,
review, and action reports that players have filed against one another. It is
the operational companion to the
[User Reporting retention settings](/docs/infra/user-reporting/config-tools/retention),
where you control how long reports are retained.

<Note>
  User Reports is available on **Auth snap v1.11 and above**. If your Auth
  snap is on an older version, the tool will prompt you to upgrade.
</Note>

## Pre-requisites

You need at least the **cluster-service-view** role with **Auth** service
access to view reports, and update access to action them. If you do not have
the required permissions, please reach out to your company administrator to
upgrade your role.

## Tool Location

From your Snapend homepage, open **User Reporting** under **Quick Links**, or
select **User Reports** from the **Tools** section of the left navigation bar.

## Searching Reports

The tool lists every report in the Snapend. You can narrow the list with the
filters at the top:

1. **State** — Unprocessed, Processing, or Resolved (or all states).
2. **Category** — a free-text match on the report category (for example
   `harassment`).
3. **Target User ID** — the player the reports were filed against.
4. **Assignee** — the agent a report is assigned to.

Set any combination of filters and click **Search**. Results are paginated, and
each row shows the report's state, category, target user, reporting user,
assignee, and creation time.

![snapser-user-reports-search](/images/docs/user-reports-search.png)

<Note>
  The reports are scoped to the environment your Snapend is running on — the
  backend resolves the environment automatically, so there is no environment
  selector on this tool.
</Note>

## Viewing User Details

Both the **Target User** and **Reported By** columns show the player's ID with
two ways to dig in:

- Click the **person icon** for a quick-view popover of that player's login
  methods and profile attributes (profile attributes require the Profiles
  snap).
- Click the **player ID** itself to open the
  [User Manager](/docs/snapend/tools/user-manager) for that player in a new tab.

![snapser-user-reports-user-details](/images/docs/user-reports-user-details.png)

## Inspecting a Report

Click **Details** on any row to expand a panel showing the full **Complaint**
and **Notes** for that report.

![snapser-user-reports-details-expand](/images/docs/user-reports-details-expand.png)

## Actioning a Report

Click **Action** on a row to open the action dialog, where you can:

1. **Change the state** — move the report between Unprocessed, Processing, and
   Resolved.
2. **Assign it** — set the assignee, or click **Assign to me** to assign it to
   your own account.
3. **Edit the notes** — record context for your team. Use **+ Add signature** to
   insert your email and today's date at the cursor.

![snapser-user-reports-action-modal](/images/docs/user-reports-action-modal.png)

<Note>
  Notes **replace** the existing value on save — they are not appended. The
  field is pre-filled with the current notes so you can edit the full text.
</Note>

Click **Save** to write your changes back to the backend.

<Note>
  Every action taken on a report is recorded in the Audit logs.
</Note>
