# About Snapser

**Snapser** offers developers the capability to create cutting-edge, customizable, and scalable backends tailored to apps, games, and digital experiences of any kind. Snapser is not a one-size-fits-all solution — you assemble your own backend from modular building blocks and seamlessly integrate custom code, data, servers, and tools to make it your own.

### Snapser Features

Snapser offers a comprehensive suite of backend services including **Authentication**, **Profiles**, **Storage**, **Social Graph**, and much more. Developers also have the flexibility to integrate their **[own code](/docs/custom/bring-your-own-snap)**, **[data plane](/docs/custom/bring-your-own-data-store)**, and **[servers](/docs/custom/bring-your-own-external-server)** seamlessly into your backend aka **[snapend](/docs/snapend)**'s, modular framework. Each Snapend includes infrastructure **[monitoring](/docs/snapend/monitoring)**, **[logging](/docs/snapend/logs)**, and a **[custom SDK generator](/docs/snapend/sdks)** for all supported platforms. Snapser provides out-of-the-box tools such as **[customer support tools](/docs/snapend/tools/user-manager)**, **[administration tools](/docs/snapend/admin-tools)**, and **[business intelligence tool](/docs/business-intelligence)** to streamline ongoing development, management and live operations.

## Infrastructure

Snapser offers an extensive suite of **backend services**, **relay compute**, **server fleets**, and a **business intelligence** platform, providing an all-in-one solution for app developers and game studios alike.

* **[Multiplayer Services](/docs/infra/snaps)** offers a comprehensive suite of generic backend and gamification services for any application.
* Manage and scale application or game servers via **[Snapser Fleet](/docs/infra/snaps/game-server-fleets)** services.
* Facilitate real-time workflows—whether cooperative apps or PvP games—using **[Snapser Relay](/docs/infra/snaps/relay)**.
* Leverage **[Snapser Customization](/docs/custom)** to bring your own code, data, and servers into your Snapser backend.
* Review and visualize analytics via Snapser’s business intelligence tool called **[Olympus](/docs/business-intelligence)**.
