# Bring your Own External Server

Bring your own external server onto Snapser.

## Concept

Some developers have an existing cloud functions or a server they have been using.
It's super easy, with BYOS, to architecturally put this behind your Snapend
gateway. The advantage of doing this is, if you have an Auth snap as part of
your Snapend then you do not have to handle Authentication and Authorization.
Instead, your gateway handles this for you and forwards the request to your
server. All you have to do is, give your server a **name** and add a forwarding domain. For security reasons we only support domains over https.

## Usage

When you are creating a new Snapend in Step number three you will be exposed to all the **Bring your owns (BYOs)**
that Snapser supports. Click the "Servers" Nav item from the left section. You will see a form that takes in a name and a domain.

It should be noted that the name has to be unique within your Snapend. If you have selected say leaderboards marketplace Snap, then you cannot name your BYOS as leaderboards.

For the domain, make sure you enter just the **domain**. You should not enter any
additional paths in this section.

![Byos Pic](/images/docs/byos.png)

## Example

Say your cluster URL is **xyzabc** and you have
added a BYOS called **custom** and the domain
you entered is **https://foo.bar**. Your server
will now be reachable at **https://gateway.snapser.com/xyzabc/v1/custom**

<Note>
  Snapser has made a design decision to always version BYOS. Hence you will find
  the final URL to have /v1/custom in the example above.
</Note>
