# Bring your Own Data Stores

Bring your own Data plane onto Snapser.

## Concept

This one is near and dear to our heart. We want our customers to have
complete control over their data. Doing this allows developers to own their
own data as well as run their ML pipelines efficiently and have full
visibility. Developers can choose to attach their own data plane while
creating a Snapend. With BYODS, even though Snapser runs the compute layer,
all the user data generated by the services would be stored on your data
plane.

## Usage

When you are creating a new Snapend in Step number three you will be exposed to all the **Bring your owns (BYOs)**
that Snapser supports. Click the "Data Stores" Nav item from the left section which will show you a list of Snaps that you have
added to your cluster. Pick the Snap you want to replace the data plane for and click the **Configure Data Stores** button.
In this form you will have to enter your data plane IP address, port and password wherever applicable. If you leave sections of the form blank. Snapser will automatically handled those stores for you.

<Note>
It should be noted that you can also use BYODS partially. For example, say you want to only own the main user data generated by the **Authentication** snap and you are fine with Snapser running the data plane for the remaining snaps.
You can just fill the section for the auth snap and the rest is handled by Snapser.
</Note>

**IMPORTANT**: Snap version upgrades also work seamlessly with your own Data Stores. Lets assume that you are using
Snapser's Authentication snap and have added BYODS for it. Say a few months later
we release a new version of this Snap that requires adding a new column to the
Database. You will not have to worry about this. Our upgrade Snapend automation
handles this case automatically. This also works if you are downgrading your
Snap.

## Supported BYO Data Stores
Snapser currently only supports swapping out Postgres and Redis data planes. We will unlock support for Couchbase in the near future.

## FAQs

### Can I bring in any data store I want as part of BYODS?

As authors of the Snaps in the marketplace, we have made decisions based on a
lot of research on the appropriate data store to use for the feature at hand.
For example, we use Redis for our Leaderboards Snap. So if you want to BYODS
your Redis Snap you will have to provide host and port of a Redis instance
itself. If you provide some other data plane the Snap will not function.

### How does BYOSnap and BYODS work?

Once you are bring your own Snap into Snapser, we enforce no restrictions on
what data plane you can use with it. For your own security you can provide
egress ports when you upload BYOSnap which will ensure that we poke the right
holes for your custom snap to have the ability to talk to your data plane.

### Can Snapser help me spin up my own data plane?

Yes, we would be happy to help you with this. We will make sure we add VPC peering so that your Snapend
can talk to your data plane. Please reach out to us and we will help you with this.
