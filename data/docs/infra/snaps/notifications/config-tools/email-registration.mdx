# Email Registration
This tool allows you to register your email domain with Snapser.
You will have to reach out to your support team to get your domain validated.

To get started:
1. Click on `Setup > Email` from the side Nav.
2. This should open up a form where you can register your sender email domain.
<Note>
  Even if you save the email domain, you will not be able to send emails until your domain is validated.
  So please reach out to your support team to get your domain validated.
</Note>

### Sender Email Details
1. **Sender Email Address**: The email address from which the notification emails will be sent. This should be a valid email address from your domain.
