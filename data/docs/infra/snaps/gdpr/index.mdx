# GDPR - CCPA

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer
Privacy Act) are two significant data privacy regulations that aim to protect
individuals' personal data and give them greater control over how their data
is collected, processed, and used by organizations.

## GDPR

GDPR is a comprehensive data protection regulation that was implemented by the
European Union (EU) to strengthen data privacy rights for individuals within
the EU and European Economic Area (EEA). It came into effect on May 25, 2018.
Key aspects of GDPR include:

1. Consent: Organizations must obtain clear and explicit consent from individuals before collecting and processing their personal data.
2. Data Breach Notification: Organizations are required to report data breaches to supervisory authorities and affected individuals within a specified time frame.
3. Right to Access: Individuals have the right to request access to their personal data that organizations hold and to receive information about how their data is being used.
4. Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): Individuals can request the deletion of their personal data in certain circumstances.
5. Data Portability: Individuals have the right to receive their personal data in a structured, machine-readable format and to transfer it to another organization.
6. Data Protection Officer (DPO): Some organizations are required to appoint a Data Protection Officer to oversee data protection activities.

## CCPA

CCPA is a data privacy law that was enacted in the state of California, United
States. It went into effect on January 1, 2020. CCPA aims to enhance privacy
rights and consumer protection for California residents. Key features of CCPA
include:

1. Right to Know: Consumers have the right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold by businesses.
2. Right to Delete: Consumers can request the deletion of their personal information held by businesses.
3. Right to Opt-Out: Consumers have the right to opt-out of the sale of their personal information.
4. Non-Discrimination: Businesses cannot discriminate against consumers who exercise their CCPA rights.
5. Data Breach Notification: Businesses are required to implement reasonable security practices and to notify consumers in the event of a data breach.

## Solution

Rather than dealing with the complexity of identifying the precise user
location, dealing with location laws etc. the best practice is to offer the
ability to get and delete all game/app data, to all users. You as a company
can totally make a decision to not offer this functionality to users in
certain regions. But we highly recommend that you offer this functionality to
all users.

<Note>
  GDPR and CCPA are extremely strict laws with very high penalties. We highly
  recommend that you consult with your legal team to ensure that you are
  compliant with these laws.
</Note>

## User Consent

Consent is something that should be offered by the app developer in the form of
Terms of Service. Developers should only create an account on Snapser if the
user gives consent.

## User Data Requests

Once your cluster has the GDPR snap, your app/game client can hit the GDPR GetPin
API. Your app or game should show this PIN to the user in your app's UI. The developer
should then show the user a UI where they can enter their own email and
the PIN and hit the GetUserDetails API to request details of all their data
stored on Snapser. Snapser will send the user an email with all the data as an
attachment.

## User Deletion Requests

Once your cluster has the GDPR snap, your app client can hit the GDPR
DeleteUserData API. It is recommended that you show a confirmation dialog to
the user, because once the data is deleted it cannot be retrieved.

## BYOSnap Integration
If you have a BYOSnap that implements the GetUser and DeleteUser hooks, you
can use the GDPR snap to manage user data in compliance with GDPR regulations.

The following hooks should be implemented in your BYOSnap:

### Get User Data hook
Implement the following endpoint in your BYOSnap that retrieves user data based on the user ID.
- Method: `GET`
- URL: `/v1/$byosnapId/settings/users/<user_id>/data`
- Response: Any JSON object containing user data.

### Delete User Data hook
Implement the following endpoint in your BYOSnap that deletes user data based on the user ID.
- Method: `DELETE`
- URL: `/v1/$byosnapId/settings/users/<user_id>/data`
- Response: Empty object
