# Custom Email Template
Custom email templates allow you to personalize verification emails sent to your users as
part of email auth. You can customize the content and design of the emails to match your game branding. You can setup templates for the following:
1. Email Login: Email sent with a OTP to the user.
2. Email Verification: Email sent to the user when they verify their email address.
3. Account Recovery: Email sent to the user when they request a password reset.

To get started:
1. Click on **Email Setup** from the side Nav
2. Pick the email template you want to update.

## Requirement
Each template needs some mandatory placeholders:

- `{{.OTP}}`: This placeholder will be replaced with the actual OTP.
- `{{.TTL}}`: This placeholder will be replaced with the time duration until the OTP remains valid.

![Custom Template Admin](/images/docs/auth-custom-template.png)

## Template Setup
The template setup takes the following inputs
1. <u>Sender</u> - This is the sender email address. Note that you need to verify your sender
   with Snapser. Please reach out to support to get your process started. It can take up to
   24 hours for us to activate your custom sender address.
2. <u>Subject</u> - Subject line for your email. Please be careful when selecting your subject line. Many email providers may mark emails as spam based on the subject line.
3. <u>Template</u> - This is the actual body of the email. It should be noted that Snapser requires
   the template to have `{{.OTP}} and {{.TTL}}`in your template. Snapser uses these variables
   in the template to populate the one-time-password and expiry at runtime.
