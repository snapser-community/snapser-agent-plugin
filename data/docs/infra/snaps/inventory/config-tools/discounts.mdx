# Discount
This tool allows you to create, update, and view discounts that can be applied to purchase and unlock costs for items and containers.

## Summary

Here is a high-level summary of the concepts in discounts:

- **Discounts**: The overarching entity that holds the eligibility criteria and formula for the discount to be applied.
- **Applicable Discounts**: Items or containers can then be assigned discount entities with a floor price and a strategy of which of the applicable discounts to pick in case of conflict.
- **Applied Discounts**: When a user purchases items or unlocks containers, they will see the discount that they were eligible for and was applied, and the price after discount application.

## Discount Details

A discount consists of the following fields:

- **Name**: A unique name is required that identifies the discount.
- **Display name**: A short name that can be displayed to users.
- **Description**: A description for the discount.
- **Eligibility criteria**: The conditions a user must satisfy in order to be eligible for a discount.
- **Formula**: The formula by which the price of an item will be reduced.

### Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility criteria consist of multiple eligibility groups, where each eligibility group consists of multiple eligibilities.
A user is considered eligible for a discount if they satisfy the requirements for ***all of the eligibilities in any of the groups***.

Each eligibility can be one of the following types:
- **Statistics based**: The user must have a certain value for a statistic, e.g., `"num_wins" >= 10`
- **Segment based**: The user must be part of or not part of a specific segment, e.g., `not in "new_players"`
- **Time range based**: The user must be within a specific time range, e.g., `between "2023-01-01" and "2023-12-31"`

### Formula

The formula defines how the discount is applied to the price of an item. It can be a simple percentage off or a fixed amount off.

- **Percentage off**: A percentage value that will be subtracted from the price, e.g., `10% off`
- **Fixed amount off**: A fixed amount that will be subtracted from the price, e.g., `10 coins off`
- **Statistics based percentage off**: A percentage value that is calculated based on a user's statistic and a denominator, e.g., `(num_wins * 100/ denominator)% off`
- **Statistics based fixed amount off**: A fixed amount that is calculated based on a user's statistic and a multiplier, e.g., `(num_wins * multiplier) coins off`

## Applying Discounts

Once a discount is created, it can be applied to items or containers.

This applies to:
- Purchase prices of items and containers
- Unlock costs of containers

Each of the above can have multiple possible prices, and each price can have multiple applicable discounts.
Hence, while applying discounts, a strategy is required to determine which discount to apply in case multiple eligibilities are fulfilled.
The strategy can be one of the following:
- **Best**: The discount that results in the lowest price will be applied.
- **Worst**: The discount that results in the highest price will be applied.

Aside from the strategy, each applicable discount can also have a floor price. This is the minimum price that can be applied after the discount is applied.
If the calculated price after applying the discount is lower than the floor price, the floor price will be used instead.

## Applied Discounts

When a user lists catalog items or containers, the purchasable settings of the discounted items will have a field `applied_discounts` populated. 
This field will contain the `price_after_discounts` based on their eligibilities. When the user purchases the items or unlocks the containers, only the price after discount will be charged.
