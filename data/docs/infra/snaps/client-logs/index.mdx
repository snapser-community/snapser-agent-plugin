# Client Logs

The Snapser Client Logs service is designed to help client developers
with debugging. There are some issues, that only show up on a device and not
in your engine editor. This service can be used to log such issues.

## Viewing Logs

After you fire off your payload to the Client logs snap you will see your data
in the Logs tool under the **Infrastructure**{' '}
Nav on your cluster page.

<Note>
It is highly recommended to use this Snap only for debugging purposes. If you want to track your errors
consider using the pre-built Analytics error event to track your errors.
</Note>
