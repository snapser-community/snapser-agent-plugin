# User Reporting

User Reporting lets your players report one another from your game client.
Each report is filed against a **target user** by a **reporting user**, with
a category and a complaint, and moves through three states as your team works
it: **Unprocessed**, **Processing**, and **Resolved**.

User Reporting is powered by the Auth snap and becomes available on **Auth
snap v1.11.1 and above**.

## How it fits together

There are two surfaces for User Reporting in your Snapend:

1. **Retention settings** — configure how long reports are kept in each state
   before they are automatically deleted. See
   [Retention](/docs/infra/user-reporting/config-tools/retention).
2. **User Reports tool** — search, review, and action the reports themselves
   (change state, assign, add notes). See
   [User Reports](/docs/snapend/tools/user-reports).

<Note>
  Reports filed against a specific player are also surfaced in the
  [User Manager](/docs/snapend/tools/user-manager) when you look that player up.
</Note>
