# Invoices

## Concept

At the close of every month Snapser generates one invoice per app, covering
everything that app consumed across all of its Snapends. The Invoices tool is
where you read those invoices — the total you were billed, how that total was
worked out, how much of your plan you used, and a per-Snapend breakdown of
where the usage actually came from.

<Note>
  Invoices are available on every Snapser plan. They are not shown on
  Enterprise self-hosted deployments, where billing is handled outside the
  portal.
</Note>

## Pre-requisites

The company-level Invoices page requires a `company-*` role with view access.
The app-level page requires a `game-*` role with view access on that app. If
you do not have the role, the page redirects you back to your landing page.

## Tool Location

There are two ways in, and they show the same data scoped differently:

* **Company level** — from your company home page, open the left nav and click
  **Tools → Invoices**. You land on a list of every app that has invoices.
* **App level** — from an app's home page, click **Tools → Invoices**. This
  skips the app picker and opens that app's invoices directly.

## Picking an app

The company-level page opens on a summary with one row per app:

1. **Game** - The app the invoices belong to.
2. **Billing month** - The most recent month Snapser has invoiced for.
3. **Last invoice** - The total on that most recent invoice.

Click a row to open that app's invoice history. Use the back arrow next to the
app name to return to the summary.

## Invoice history

Once you are inside an app you get its month-by-month history:

* A **Total monthly spend** bar chart, one bar per billing month, so you can
  see the trend at a glance. It only appears once the app has more than one
  invoice.
* A table of every invoice, listing the **Billing month** and the **Total**.

Click any row to open the full invoice.

## Reading an invoice

The invoice header shows the app name, the exact date range the invoice
covers, and the **Total billed**. Use the back arrow in the header to return to
the invoice history.

![Invoices](/images/docs/invoices.png)

### Cost composition

A donut chart showing what share of the total each charge accounts for.
Charges that came to zero are left out, so the chart only shows what you
actually paid for.

### Plan usage

Next to the chart, newer invoices show how much of your plan you used during
the month, with your plan tier alongside:

1. **Seats**
2. **BI seats**
3. **Snaps**
4. **Snapends**
5. **Apps**

Each one shows what you used against your plan's limit. The bar turns amber
once you pass 90% of a limit and red once you exceed it.

### Cost breakdown

The breakdown table is split into sections. Each section ends with its own
total, and the invoice **Total** sits at the bottom.

**Usage** - Charges that vary with what your app did this month:

1. **Game Server Fleets** - Compute and bandwidth for your game server fleets.
2. **BYOSnap** - Compute for the custom snaps you brought to the platform.
3. **Storage** - Data stored across your Snapends.
4. **Telemetry** - Logs, metrics and traces.
5. **Matchmaking** - Matchmaking usage.
6. **Non-Production Usage** - Usage accrued on your development and staging
   Snapends.
7. **Production Usage** - See [How production usage is billed](#how-production-usage-is-billed)
   below.

**Platform and Features** - Your recurring plan charges:

1. **Platform Fee** - The recurring fee for your plan.
2. **Features (Support, etc.)** - Plan features such as your support tier.
3. **Discounts** - Any discounts on your platform fee or features, taken off
   before the section total.

**Other Discounts** - Any remaining discounts, such as usage discounts, listed
by discount code. This section only appears when one applies.

Click a row with an arrow to see how it was worked out — the quantity, the
rate, and the resulting amount. Rows that include a discount open
automatically, so money taken off is never hidden.

### How production usage is billed

Your production usage is charged as the **higher** of two numbers, never the
sum of both:

* **Snap floor** - the committed usage amount for your plan.
* **HAU Usage** - what you metered this month, measured in Hosted Active
  Users. The label on this line follows the usage metric in your contract.

The **Production Usage** row shows the amount actually charged. Expand it to
see both numbers side by side: the one that was billed, and the other struck
through, with a note saying which won this period. Only the billed amount
contributes to your total.

### Per-Snapend breakdown

The last section attributes the month's usage to individual Snapends, so you
can tell which environment drove the bill. Production Snapends are listed
first, then staging, then development, each tagged with its environment. A
Snapend that was deleted during the month is still listed, marked
**Deleted**.

For each Snapend you get:

1. **Days** - How many days of the month the Snapend was billed for.
2. **Game Servers** - Game server fleet compute and bandwidth.
3. **BYOSnap** - Custom snap compute and bandwidth.
4. **Storage** - Data stored.
5. **Telemetry** - Logs, metrics and traces.
6. **Matchmaking** - Matchmaking usage.
7. **Production Usage** or **HAU** - The billed production usage. It only
   appears on your production Snapend, since that is the only one it is
   charged to. The column is named after whichever side won this period.
8. **Total** - Everything attributed to that Snapend.

Click a Snapend to see the raw quantities behind its costs, such as vCPU-hours,
GiB transferred, GB-days stored and telemetry events. The **Total attributed**
row at the bottom adds up every Snapend.

<Note>
  The per-Snapend breakdown covers usage only. Your platform fee, features
  and discounts are not attributed to any Snapend, so **Total attributed**
  will not always match the invoice total.
</Note>

## Paying your invoice

The Invoices tool is read-only — it is where you review what you were charged,
not where you pay. How you pay depends on your plan:

* **Annual contract** - Snapser emails you the invoice along with a payment
  link. There is nothing to set up in the portal.
* **On-Demand** - You need a credit card on file. Add or update it under
  **Settings → Billing**, and Snapser charges that card each month.
