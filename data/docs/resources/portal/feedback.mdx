# Feedback

Snapser is built with its customers, not just for them. The **Feedback** tool lets you send
feature requests, bug reports, and notes on how a tool feels to use, straight from the Web App
to the Snapser team.

Every submission becomes a ticket that our team triages. You do not need to open a separate support
thread or email anymore.

<Warning>
Feedback is available on the Premium and Enterprise tiers.
</Warning>

## Sending feedback

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner of the Web App.
2. Select **Feedback** from the menu.
3. Fill in the form and click **Submit**.

![snapser-feedback](/images/docs/portal-feedback.png)

You will see a confirmation with a reference for your submission. Keep it if you want to
mention the request in a later conversation with our team.

## The form

| Field | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Type** | `Feature` for something you want built, `Bug` for something broken, `UX` for something that works but is awkward to use. |
| **Page** | The page the feedback is about. Snapser fills this in with the page you came from. Edit it if the feedback is about somewhere else. |
| **Title** | A one-line summary. Up to 120 characters. |
| **Description** | The detail. Up to 5000 characters. |

<Note>
  Open the tool from the page you want to talk about. Snapser then fills in the **Page** field
  for you, and our team knows exactly where to look.
</Note>

## Attachments

A screenshot or a screen recording usually explains a problem faster than a paragraph.

- **Paste** an image or a video directly into the **Description** field. It appears as a chip
  below the form.
- Or click **Attach** and pick a file.

You can attach up to 3 files, each up to 50 MB. Images and video only.

<Note>
  Wait for each attachment to finish uploading before you submit. **Submit** stays disabled
  while an upload is in flight. Remove an attachment by clicking the cross on its chip.
</Note>

## What we attach for you

To save you describing your setup, each submission carries some context about where it came
from:

- The page you were on
- Your company, app, and Snapend Ids
- Your email address
- The Web App version and environment

## UX feedback

The `UX` type exists because we want to hear about the things that are not bugs. A flow that
takes too many clicks, a label that reads wrong, a table that hides the column you need — these
never surface in a support ticket, and they are the changes that make the Web App better to
live in day to day.

## FAQs

### Who can send feedback?

Anyone on your team with a Snapser account, as long as your company is on the Premium or
Enterprise tier. There is no separate permission or [access role](/docs/resources/portal/access)
to assign.

### Will someone reply to me?

Feedback is a one-way channel into our backlog, not a support queue. Our team reads and triages
everything, but you will not get a reply in the tool. For anything that needs an answer, use
your usual support channel.

### Is this the right place for an outage?

No. Feedback goes to a product backlog. If your Snapend is down or degraded, use your support
channel so it reaches an on-call engineer.

### What happens to my screenshots?

Attachments are stored with the ticket and are visible only to the Snapser team. They are not
public, and the links do not work outside our issue tracker.
